Mark Bjornsgaard is an entrepreneur, investor and systems thinker who has spent the last two decades dismantling one of business's most cherished myths: that innovation is driven by ideas.

As founder of System Two, he has built and backed companies across energy, healthcare, finance and technology, doubling revenues year after year and exiting multiple ventures. His work is grounded in practice, not theory — running experiments, killing bad assumptions early and replacing innovation theatre with evidence.

Best known for his work on Deep Green and his book *The Idea Drug*, Mark helps organisations move faster, waste less and make progress without pretending certainty.
