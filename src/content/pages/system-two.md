## How we work

Ideas are treated with suspicion. Hypotheses are treated seriously. We set up ventures, test assumptions, and live with the consequences. The result: doubled revenues year after year for over a decade.

We operate as builders, not consultants. We've worked at board level with British Gas, Barclays, Experian, and GSK — always with skin in the game.

## Because > Someone > Will

Every venture begins with a need-state hypothesis. We force ourselves to declare the behavioural driver (**Because**), identify a specific persona (**Someone**), and only then describe the solution (**Will**).
