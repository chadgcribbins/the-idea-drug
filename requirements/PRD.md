## 1. Overview

### Vision
A full personal brand website for Mark Bjornsgaard, centred around the launch of his book **"The Idea Drug"** (published by Lowell & Pearce). The site serves a dual purpose: it is the primary conversion engine for book sales and email capture, while also establishing Mark's authority as a venture builder through his firm **System Two**.

### Subtitle
"Why Ideas Are Killing Innovation and How to Stop Using Them."

### Core Thesis
Ideas are a drug — the default output of our fast, impulsive System One brain. They feel productive but they're solutions in disguise, hijacking attention away from customer need. The book introduces a forcing function — the **Need-state Hypothesis** (Because > Someone > Will) — to restore the scientific method to innovation. It's not a theory; it's a field-tested operating system (HypothesisOS) for building things people actually want.

The site should feel like James Clear's site — clean, editorial, confident, square. No fluff. The design communicates credibility and substance through restraint. The cover does the screaming (vivid red, gritty, bold type); the site channels that energy through red accents on a clean cream canvas.

### Tagline / Brand Mark
**"Because Someone Will"** — accompanied by a smiley face icon motif.

### Book Structure (for content planning)
The book is divided into three parts and is the **first in a planned trilogy**:
- **Part 1 — Why**: Why most innovation fails, critiques of idea-led approaches (including Lean Startup's Build-Measure-Learn skipping the hypothesis step)
- **Part 2 — What**: The Need-state Hypothesis (Because > Someone > Will) with worked examples
- **Part 3 — How**: "Rinsing" — a 4-6 week field manual for defining, testing, and killing hypotheses

Future volumes: Vol 2 (running sprints with hypothesis definition), Vol 3 (scaling or killing ventures).

### Key Terminology
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Because > Someone > Will** | The hypothesis triplet: behavioural driver → persona → solution |
| **Rinsing** | The 4-6 week intensive process for "cleaning and killing hypotheses" — exposure therapy for breaking the ideas addiction |
| **HypothesisOS** | Brand term for the methodology as an operating system upgrade |
| **Need-state Hypothesis** | A tight, falsifiable statement about why a specific group will change their behaviour |
| **System One / System Two** | Kahneman's dual-process theory — fast/impulsive vs slow/logical thinking |
| **The KLF** | The book's tonal inspiration — the KLF's original "The Manual" (a deadpan, brutal guide to having a #1 hit single) |

## 6. Design System

### Reference
All design tokens are defined in `project.tokens.json` (already created, sourced from jamesclear.com with corrections).

### Key Design Principles

| Principle | Implementation |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Square and sharp** | All border-radius: 0px. No rounded corners anywhere. |
| **Restrained palette** | Cream background (#F9F8F4), black text (#000000), black buttons, minimal colour. The book cover provides the colour accent. |
| **Editorial typography** | Europa for display/headings, system fonts for body. Large, confident type sizes. |
| **Generous whitespace** | Sections breathe. No cramming. Let the content and typography do the work. |
| **Alternating sections** | White and light grey (#f3f4f6) backgrounds to create visual rhythm between page sections. |
| **Dark header** | Dark charcoal/green navigation bar consistent across all pages. |

### Component Patterns (from wireframes)

- **Two-column with divider**: Left content block + vertical rule + right sidebar (used for About snippet, A Team section)
- **CTA rows**: Label text on left + black square button on right (used for S2 engagement options)
- **Card grid**: Image + text for portfolio items (investments, ventures, VD projects)
- **Email capture**: Inline form — square text input + black submit button, side by side
- **Retailer button row**: Horizontal row of equally-sized black buttons (Amazon, Audible, Spotify, Apple)
- **Step funnel**: Numbered steps with alternating white/grey backgrounds (Step 1: Buy, Step 2: Register, Step 3: Get copy)

## 7. Email & Lead Capture Strategy

### Recommended Platform: Kit (ConvertKit)

**Why Kit:**
- Built for creators/authors — this is their core use case
- Visual automation builder for drip sequences
- Easy-to-embed forms that match custom styling
- Tagged subscriber management (segment by lead magnet: free chapter vs. templates vs. S2 pre-read)
- Landing pages for gated downloads (Chapter 1, pre-read PDFs)
- Good Zapier/webhook integrations

### Lead Magnets (5 capture points)

| Lead Magnet | Location | Tag/Segment |
|-------------|----------|-------------|
| **Free Chapter 1 download** | Home hero, Book page | `book-chapter-download` |
| **Templates registration** | Book page (Step 2) | `book-templates` |
| **S2 Pre-read PDF** | S2 page, Home S2 panel | `s2-pre-read` |
| **Readiness download** | S2 page | `s2-readiness` |
| **General newsletter** | Footer (all pages) | `newsletter` |

### Email Automation Sequences

1. **Book chapter download** → Welcome email with PDF → Day 2: "Here's why Chapter 3 will change how you think about ideas" → Day 5: "Ready for the full book?" (buy links) → Day 10: Templates offer
2. **S2 enquiry** → Confirmation → Pre-read attachment → Day 3: Case study → Day 7: "Book a taster day" CTA
3. **Post-purchase (template registrants)** → Templates delivery → Ongoing value emails → Workshop invitations

### Form Design
- Single field (email only) for speed
- Square input + black button, inline layout
- Specific button text per context: "Download the FREE Chapter" / "Register" / "Download our pre-read"
- Privacy note beneath: "No spam. Unsubscribe anytime."

## Appendix: Design Token Reference

The visual language is defined in `project.tokens.json` in the project root. Key values:

- **Brand primary**: `#D42020` (cover red — accent colour for highlights, links, focus states, dividers)
- **Brand accent**: `#A8153A` (deep crimson — darker red from cover texture, secondary accent)
- **Brand secondary**: `#F9F8F4` (warm cream — page background for readability)
- **Buttons**: `#000000` (black bg + white text, square, 0px radius — matches the bold "DRUG" typography treatment)
- **Text**: `#000000` on cream
- **Display font**: Europa (bold, condensed — echoing the cover's impactful type)
- **Body font**: System font stack
- **All border-radius**: 0px (square everything — aligns with cover's hard edges)
- **Shadows**: Minimal — natural (6px 6px 9px rgba(0,0,0,0.2)) used sparingly

**Design rationale**: The cover is aggressive — vivid red, gritty texture, massive type. The site should channel that energy through the **colour palette and typography weight** while maintaining a **clean editorial layout** for usability. Red is used for accents (dividers, focus rings, hover states, links) not as background fills. The black buttons echo the cover's "DRUG" text treatment. The cream background provides contrast and breathing room — the cover already does the heavy lifting as a visual centrepiece on the page.
